01 / Profile

About Me

I am a Paris-based Senior Geospatial Data Scientist, Earth Observation (EO) expert, and Independent Consultant with a focus on environmental risk analysis.

My career bridges corporate hazard risk analysis, academic research, and international climate change adaptation initiatives. I specialize in utilizing satellite remote sensing data and advanced spatial modeling techniques to quantify environmental hazards, assess climate risks, and build data-driven tools for sustainable development.

I hold an MSc in Geographical Information Science from the University of Southampton, where my research centered on global predictive wildfire modeling using fuzzy logic systems. I am currently active as a PlanAdapt Fellow, collaborating on urban heat resilience and climate action projects globally.
