01 / Profile

About Me

I am an Independent Consultant and Technical Advisor based in Paris, working in Earth Observation and Geospatial Analytics with a focus on climate and environmental issues. My work includes climate risk modelling, such as developing vulnerability functions for flood, wind, and fire risks to help with financial decisions. I also work on multi-hazard spatial analysis, canopy change detection, early warning systems, and building reproducible geospatial data pipelines that follow FAIR principles.

My career combines experience in corporate consulting, academic research, and international climate change adaptation projects. I specialise in using satellite remote sensing data and advanced spatial modelling to measure environmental hazards, assess climate risks, and create data-driven tools for sustainable development.

I have an MSc in Geographical Information Science from the University of Southampton. My research there focused on global predictive wildfire modelling using fuzzy logic systems. For more details about my ongoing professional development, please visit my Training & Resources page.

I am currently a PlanAdapt Fellow, working on urban heat resilience and climate action projects around the world. As a Technical Advisor, I also contribute to the teams at Green Box, AstraTerra, and Blu-H.

02 / Experience

Professional Activities

Collaborating with international research networks, insurers, and development organisations to deliver geospatial solutions.

Independent Geospatial Consulting

Providing custom spatial data pipeline engineering, Earth Observation analytics, and climate vulnerability modelling for international organisations, commercial insurers, and research entities. Key focus areas include:

  • Earth Observation (EO) & Remote Sensing: Developing automated cloud-native processing pipelines (Google Earth Engine, AWS) for agricultural parcel monitoring, multi-sensor canopy change detection, early warning alert systems, and scalable land-cover classification.
  • Urban Land Use Planning: Designing GIS-based spatial decision-support platforms that integrate statutory zoning, planning policy, and high-resolution environmental datasets (soils, elevation, land cover) for urban agriculture suitability, land degradation neutrality, and sustainable town and country planning.
  • Catastrophe Risk Modelling: Led GIS research, spatial analysis, and flood/wildfire hazard mapping projects within modelling divisions serving the European insurance market, both as an employed researcher and later through independent consulting engagements.

Pro-Bono Technical Advisory Work

Advising early-stage ventures, non-profits, and networks on geospatial data operations, Earth Observation, and smart systems architecture (digital twins) — including Green Box (tropical forest protection, indigenous community empowerment, and transparent carbon/biodiversity markets), Blu-H (humanitarian environmental intelligence, satellite-based flood monitoring), AstraTerra (space, smart cities, and connected communities consultancy), and PlanAdapt (urban climate resilience, including the COOL4ALL initiative).
